Expanding expressions

Fundamentally, multiplying the factors one by one is the most effective and error-free method.

$$ \begin{align*} c(a+b)&=ca+cb\\ (a+b)^2&=a^2+2ab+b^2\\ (a-b)^2&=a^2-2ab+b^2\\ (a+b)(a-b)&=a^2-b^2 \end{align*} $$

Symmetric polynomials (1)

A symmetric polynomial is a polynomial which remains the same after interchanging the variables. For example, consider $$(a+b+c)(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca).$$ Since expanding it results in \(a^3\), we can predict that \(b^3\) and \(c^3\) must also appear and their respective coefficients are equal.
